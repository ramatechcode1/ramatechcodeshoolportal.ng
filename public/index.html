MODULE 04 — WHAT YOUR SCHOOL PROVIDES

A classroom or lab

Clean, secure, well-ventilated — computers optional, students can work in small groups.

Stable power

A reliable supply for practical sessions; an inverter or generator is a bonus, not a requirement.

A timetable slot

Once weekly, after school, during a club period, or on weekends — whatever fits your calendar.

A liaison teacher

One ICT coordinator or teacher to coordinate with our instructors.

Secure storage

A safe place to keep robotics kits and components between sessions.

Help with enrollment

Inform parents and register interested students — the portal handles the rest.
